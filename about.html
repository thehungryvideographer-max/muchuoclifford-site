Who I Am

Hi, I'm
Muchuo Clifford

For over 5 years, behind the camera and in the edit room, telling stories that move people to act.

“I am committed to using digital media to improve the digital communication of brands, mentor aspiring creatives, create social impact, and contribute to the UN Sustainable Development Goals through creative storytelling and Visuals You Can't Resist.”

For over 5 years I've helped small businesses, NGOs, artists and families tell their stories through video, photography and design. From conferences and birthdays to brand campaigns and humanitarian films, I make visuals that people stop to watch.

Alongside the creative work, I teach young people how to stay safe online, help victims of scams, and recover hacked or stolen accounts, because a strong brand also needs to be a safe one.
